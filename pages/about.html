Her Economy

Advancing Women Financial Inclusion for a Prosperous Tomorrow

Event Description

HER Economy is an annual Women, Gender and Financial Inclusion summit established in 2023. Now hosted by the Africa Women Financial Inclusion Initiative (AWFII), in partnership with the Graça Machel Trust's New Faces New Voices (South African Chapter), the convening provides a high-level platform to address the persistent gender financial gap.

HER Economy 2026 will convene senior policymakers, regulators, development finance institutions, investors, financial institutions, corporates, and women leaders to advance women-centred financial infrastructure, mobilise partnerships and capital commitments, and position Africa as a leader in women's financial inclusion systems.

The programme includes strategic plenaries, closed-door roundtables, evidence and innovation showcases, and a Gala & Awards evening recognising leadership reshaping the architecture of ownership.

New Faces New Voice South Africa Launch | 9 May 2025

We were excited to have officially launched the New Faces New Voices Women in Finance South Africa chapter! This pan-African network is committed to expanding the role and influence of women in the financial sector by bridging the funding gap, advocating for policy reform, and accelerating women's access to financial services. "The overall objective of the network is to advance the financial inclusion of women by bringing more women into the formal financial system."

Women Financial Inclusion Summit

Theme: "Advancing Women Financial Inclusion for a Prosperous Tomorrow"

7 May 2026 | VENUE: Johannesburg

Event Objectives

  • Advocate and amplify the narrative for financial inclusion of women in Africa.
  • Empower women with tools and information that will enable them to start accumulating assets, generating income, managing risks, and fully participating in the economy.
  • Networking and fostering connections.

Who Can Attend?

Her Economy 2025 is where financial inclusion meets action. Whether you're a corporate leader, entrepreneur, policymaker, or advocate, there's a place for you in this conversation.

  • Women Entrepreneurs and Business Leaders
  • Young Professionals and Aspiring Business Owners
  • Financial Professionals
  • Policy Makers and Government Officials
  • Tech Innovators
  • Financial Inclusion Advocacy Groups
  • Financial Inclusion and Gender Equality Academics and Researchers
  • Financial Literacy Educators
