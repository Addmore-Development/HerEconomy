Her Economy: Our Stakeholders & Partners

The Her Economy fosters collaboration among diverse stakeholders to ensure the successful implementation of its financial inclusion strategies. Each group brings unique insights and resources that are crucial for driving economic empowerment, access to finance, and leadership for women across the continent. By engaging with these stakeholders effectively, we create partnerships that align with our broader goals of sustainability, economic transformation, and inclusive growth.
