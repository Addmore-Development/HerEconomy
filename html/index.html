Who Should Attend HER Economy 2026?

Who Should Attend?

HER Economy 2026 is designed for senior decision-makers and practitioners, including:

  • Policymakers and regulators
  • Financial institutions and DFIs
  • Investors and capital market actors
  • Corporate leaders
  • Women leaders and entrepreneurs

Is This Event Open to the Public?

Attendance is curated but open. Tickets are available publicly; however, participation in closed-door sessions is by invitation.

Ticketing Information

Are tickets refundable?
Tickets are non-refundable. However, substitutions are allowed up to 7 days before the event.

Can I transfer my ticket?
Yes. Ticket transfers are permitted by notifying the organisers in writing at least 7 days prior to the event.

What Does My Ticket Include?

  • Two days of summit programming
  • Catering and networking
  • Access to the Gala & Awards evening

Travel & Accommodation

Delegates are responsible for their own travel and accommodation.

Closed-Door Roundtables

Closed-door roundtables are invitation-only and curated to ensure strategic dialogue. Access is based on relevance and capacity.

Discounts & Partnerships

Will there be discounted tickets?
Discounted tickets are limited to Early Bird availability only to maintain the quality of the convening.

Partner Tickets
Complimentary or partner tickets are managed directly by the organisers in line with partnership agreements.

Be Part of the Movement

Her Economy 2025 is where financial inclusion meets action. Whether you're a corporate leader, entrepreneur, policymaker, or advocate, there's a place for you in this conversation.

Event Objectives

  • Advocate and amplify the narrative for financial inclusion of women in Africa.
  • Empower women with tools and information that will enable them to start accumulating assets, generating income, managing risks, and fully participating in the economy.
  • Networking and fostering connections.

Who Can Attend?

  • Women Entrepreneurs and Business Leaders
  • Young Professionals and Aspiring Business Owners
  • Financial Professionals
  • Policy Makers and Government Officials
  • Tech Innovators
  • Financial Inclusion Advocacy Groups
  • Financial Inclusion and Gender Equality Academics and Researchers
  • Financial Literacy Educators
